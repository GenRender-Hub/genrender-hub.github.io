Video Models as Generative Renderers

Foundations, Interfaces, Systems, and Beyond

Just as classical graphics separates scene construction from rendering, modern video models are shifting from open-ended content invention toward faithful conditional rendering—preserving externally constructed geometry, rules, and cast identity while synthesizing sunlit surfaces, cloth, and secondary motion.
